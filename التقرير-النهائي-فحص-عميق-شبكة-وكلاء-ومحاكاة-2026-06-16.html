2 · 🤖 شبكة وكلاء التدقيق — أهمّ 10 نتائج مؤكَّدة

من 140 نتيجة أُكِّدت عدائيًّا، بعد التجميع والإسقاط (≈10 إيجابيّة/مُصلَحة سابقًا، وبند KYC=سليم-بالتصميم): 5 عالية · 9 متوسّطة · 9 منخفضة · 0 حرجة.

#الخطورةالدورالنتيجة + الأثر + المقترحالموضع
#1 HIGH customer Checkout shows zero delivery fee yet allows submit when quote fails (CUST-01/02 break)
Fee fallback to 0 at L187 plus a submit guard that goes false on quote failure at L1078 means the shown total is subtotal only while the backend charges the real fee, breaking the preview-equals-charge contract.
↳ مقترح: Disable submit until the fee is known unless self-pickup; warn explicitly on failure; stop defaulting the previewed fee to 0.
 web/src/pages/customer/CheckoutPage.tsx:187, 1078
#2 HIGH driver Proof shows success while order silently held in PROOF_REVIEW_REQUIRED on geofence failure
Backend holds the order when the driver is far from the destination; the frontend sets proofDone on any 200 and never reads review_status or geofence_valid, so the driver thinks delivery is done and OPS gets a false-positive review queue.
↳ مقترح: Read review_status and geofence_valid; show a review-pending screen; warn before submit when far from the address.
 web/src/pages/driver/DriverDelivery.tsx:185, 194, 511
#3 MEDIUM provider Public catalog item endpoint leaks PENDING_VERIFICATION provider menu
list_provider_catalog at L50 to L55 refuses non-active providers with 404, but get_catalog_item at L169 to L187 has no provider.status check and returns the full item with variants for a pending provider to any guest who knows the ids. Regression from Phase 450-W1-4.
↳ مقترح: Add a provider.status ACTIVE guard returning 404 in get_catalog_item or get_item_with_availability.
 backend/app/modules/catalog/catalog_router.py:169-187
#4 HIGH driver Cash online-replay 409 surfaced as a hard error after cash already collected
Backend enforces one CashCollection per order (dispatch_service.py L1456 to L1460). The frontend treats the error as success only when offline or NETWORK_ERROR (L233 to L237); a 409 replay arriving while online sets cashError at L240 and blocks the driver even though the cash was already collected.
↳ مقترح: Treat the 409 already-collected replay as success regardless of online state; disable the button fully after first success.
 web/src/pages/driver/DriverDelivery.tsx:230-241
#5 HIGH customer Wallet balance shown with no held versus available split
reserve_wallet immediately debits balance_minor (wallet_service.py L272) and the wallets/me endpoint returns only balance_minor (payment_router.py L167) with no held field, so reserved funds are invisible. Causes confusion, not money loss.
↳ مقترح: Add held_minor and available_minor to the wallet API and display available versus held distinctly.
 web/src/pages/customer/WalletPage.tsx:236-240
#6 HIGH provider PROVIDER_STAFF cannot view the ledger endpoint though they act on orders (RBAC asymmetry)
The me/ledger endpoint is gated PROVIDER_ADMIN only at L364 while orders and complaints accept ADMIN and STAFF. Settlement filtering by owned provider is correct at L1766. This is a visibility and design issue, not a leak; impact is limited because accept and reject are also admin-only today.
↳ مقترح: Add PROVIDER_STAFF to the role list at L364 to match orders and complaints, or document admin-only by design.
 backend/app/modules/providers/provider_router.py:364, 1758-1804
#7 MEDIUM provider Complaint filing has no duplicate or idempotency enforcement (spam and DoS vector)
The idempotency middleware skips the complaints route; open_complaint logs the idempotency key at L133 but never checks for an existing active complaint before INSERT at L140 to L151, and there is no unique constraint. Many rows per order spam OPS and repeatedly freeze provider settlement.
↳ مقترح: Reject when an active complaint exists for the same order, opener, and non-closed state, or add the complaints route to the idempotency allowlist.
 backend/app/modules/operations/complaints_service.py:91-151
#8 HIGH driver Cash-before-proof prerequisite communicated only via a small grey hint
Proof submit is correctly disabled for cash orders until cash is collected, but the only affordance is small grey hint text, unlike the prominent offline banner at L309 to L325. Under stress the driver thinks the button is broken. UX gap, no functional impact.
↳ مقترح: Replace the grey hint with a blocking amber or red card above the proof form plus a lock icon on the disabled button.
 web/src/pages/driver/DriverDelivery.tsx:492-500
#9 MEDIUM driver Cash outstanding not refreshed after settlement (up to 30 second stale balance)
The backend correctly decrements cash_outstanding_minor at L72 to L74, but DriverHome polls every 30 seconds with no SSE or optimistic update, so the card shows the old outstanding after settlement. State is correct; the gap is UI freshness.
↳ مقترح: After the settlement confirm response, optimistically refresh the balance in DriverHome or emit an SSE driver-balance event.
 backend/app/modules/dispatch/cash_reconcile.py:72-74
#10 MEDIUM driver Driver auto-blocked by cash limit gets zero feedback (silent dispatch starvation)
create_offer raises DRIVER_CASH_LIMIT_EXCEEDED at L236 to L242, but the auto-dispatch path catches it broadly and only warning-logs around L441 to L443, so an over-limit driver who toggles available receives no offers and no explanation.
↳ مقترح: On auto-block emit an SSE DISPATCH_BLOCKED event with reason and amounts and render a settle-cash-first card on DriverHome.
 backend/app/modules/dispatch/dispatch_service.py:235-242, 434-444

3 · 🎯 التقاطع: المحاكاة + التدقيق اتّفقتا

الاكتشاف الأقوى (مؤكَّد من زاويتين): «تجويع التوزيع الصامت بحدّ النقد». المحاكاة رصدت 16 طلبًا انتهت NO_OFFER لأنّ السائقين تجاوزوا حدّ النقد دون تسوية. والتدقيق وجد السبب في الكود (#10، dispatch_service.py:235-242,434-444): مسار التوزيع التلقائيّ يلتقط DRIVER_CASH_LIMIT_EXCEEDED ويُسجّله تحذيرًا فقط ⇒ السائق المحظور لا يتلقّى أيّ عروض ولا تفسير. الضابط صحيح (حماية مخاطر، لم يُخترَق)، لكنّ غياب الإشعار/التصعيد يحوّله إلى عنق زجاجة صامت. المقترح: بثّ حدث DISPATCH_BLOCKED + بطاقة «سوِّ نقدك أولًا» في DriverHome + رؤية OPS للطلبات العالقة.

4 · ✅ ما هو سليم (إيجابيّات مؤكَّدة)

  • لا عيب ماليّ: سيناريوهات الصرف المزدوج (محفظة الاستلام الذاتيّ · أموال المحفظة المحجوزة) محميّة بقيود UNIQUE وخصم فوريّ.
  • الأمن صامد: 5/5 فحوص BOLA/IDOR مرفوضة وقت التشغيل · الملكيّة وRBAC مُطبَّقة على الحدود.
  • القلب الماليّ مختوم · لا أخطاء خلفيّة في السجلّ · لوحات OPS بلا انجراف.
  • بند KYC=سليم بالتصميم: require_active_role يفرض Role.status=ACTIVE الذي تضبطه OPS فقط بعد اعتماد كل وثائق KYC.
  • ≈10 من النتائج كانت إيجابيّة أو مُصلَحة سابقًا (هرميّة RBAC · ترقيم الشكاوى · تصحيح مسار الاحتيال · أزرار OPS مُقيَّدة بالدور).

5 · 🧵 الأنماط المتكرّرة

  • Frontend trust and contract gaps dominate: UIs show success or a concrete number while backend state differs (zero-fee checkout, proof success during geofence hold, stale balances, replay 409 shown as error); backend logic is generally correct.
  • A silent-failure and no-feedback pattern recurs across roles: cash-limit dispatch starvation, geofence review hold, settlement balance lag, GPS required late.
  • Authorization consistency: one public leak (catalog item endpoint missing the status gate its sibling has) plus a provider-staff ledger RBAC asymmetry; both are a gate applied to one endpoint but not its twin.
  • Input-validation and spam hardening: complaint idempotency, against_type enum, reject reason max_length are all low-to-medium hardening, not breaches.
  • No confirmed financial defect: double-spend scenarios (self-pickup wallet, wallet held funds) are guarded by UNIQUE constraints and immediate-debit semantics.
  • Dedup and drop: about 10 of 30 are positive or already-resolved; the KYC accept-gate finding is correct-by-design via require_active_role enforcing Role.status ACTIVE set after KYC approval.

6 · 🔑 إصلاح إظهار كلمة المرور (منفَّذ + منشور)

ملاحظتك مُعالَجة: رُفِعت مدّة بقاء كلمة المرور ظاهرة من 60ث → 180ث (3 دقائق) + قابلة للتعديل لكل حقل (autoHideMs) في PasswordInput (يستخدمه تسجيل الدخول والتسجيل). البوّابة خضراء ونُشِر — حيّ ومؤكَّد على tawanow.com (الثابت 180000 في الحزمة المخدومة).

  2. عقود الثقة الحرجة (واجهة): #1 رسم التوصيل صفر عند فشل العرض · #2 «نجاح» الإثبات أثناء حجز geofence · #4 إعادة 409 النقد كخطأ.
  3. الشفافيّة والتغذية: #10 تجويع حدّ النقد (بثّ + بطاقة) · #5 المحفظة محجوز/متاح · #8/#9 تحسينات السائق.
  4. التقسية: #6 RBAC دفتر الموظّف · #7 منع تكرار الشكاوى (idempotency).
